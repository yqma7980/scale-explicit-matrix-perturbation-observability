# Paper 2 and Paper 3 non-overlap lock

Paper 2 develops and validates a reference-level, coordinate-explicit, route-specific finite-precision response enclosure for small dense systems and two archived finite-element tangents.

Paper 3 alone owns:

- scalable sparse inverse or response-error estimation;
- controlled large-system sequences;
- blind sparse transfer;
- coverage-tightness-cost tradeoffs for a production-oriented estimator;
- complexity and memory claims beyond the offline reference implementation.

G2-20 may derive and validate the response-route reference enclosure. It may not import a Paper 3 estimator, theorem, benchmark, result, or performance claim.
